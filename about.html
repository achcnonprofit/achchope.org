About ACHC

Built by people who refused to look away.

We are engineers, organizers, and advocates who believe technology, community, and human connection can save lives. The current system is not enough. So we are building what comes next.

01

Prevention, not just response.

Most mental health tools meet people in crisis. We are building the system that catches them weeks before they get there, by reading the signals they are already giving off.

02

The people who already love you.

You do not need another stranger to call. You need the person already in your life to know it might be time to check in. We mobilize the support network that already exists.

03

Built with privacy as a foundation, not an afterthought.

Your data is yours. You choose what is shared, with whom, and when. You can turn it off at any time. We do not sell anything, ever.

04

Designed for young adults, by young adults.

Our team is the same age as the people we are building for. We know what it feels like to be 22 and unable to ask for help. The product is shaped by that.

In memory

For Alexander.

This organization was founded in memory of Alexander Chung, whose loss inspired us to build something that could help others before they reach their darkest moment.

Everything we build is in service of the people we can still reach.
