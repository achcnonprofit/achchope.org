Get involved

There is a way for everyone to show up.

You do not need a clinical degree or a fundraising background. You need to care, and want to do something about it. Here is where you can plug in.

01
Volunteer

Become a peer supporter.

The most direct way to save a life is to be there when someone needs to talk. Our peer supporters are trained in QPR (Question, Persuade, Refer), the gold-standard short-form gatekeeper training in suicide prevention.

  • 4 to 6 hours of initial training, fully online
  • Flexible hours, fits around work or school
  • Ongoing supervision and community of supporters
  • Available to college students, working adults, anyone 18+
Apply now →
02
Build

Start a chapter.

Bring ACHC to your city or campus. We provide the technology, the training playbook, and the brand. You bring the local community, energy, and the people who will use it.

  • Full chapter launch kit and onboarding
  • Access to the AI tracker for your community
  • Peer supporter training for your local leads
  • Connected to a national network of chapters
Learn more →
03
Share

Spread the word.

Sometimes the most powerful thing you can do is make sure the right person finds us at the right moment. Share ACHC with one person who needs it, or one person who might fund it.

  • Share our story on social, with one specific person in mind
  • Forward our donation page to your network
  • Talk to your school, employer, or community about our mission
Share ACHC →
04
Fund

Donate.

Every dollar goes toward building the AI tracker, training peer supporters, and reaching communities that need it most. We are a nonprofit and operate lean: no marketing budgets, no executive salaries, no fluff.
