Our tools

The system we are building.

The AI tracker is the heart of ACHC. Everything else, the chatbot, Letters of Hope, the peer network, exists to surround it. Together they create an early warning system, a safety net, and a way for the people who love you to actually help.

Core product

AI Early Warning Tracker

The reason ACHC exists. A two-minute daily check-in feeds an AI model that learns your personal baseline and flags sustained patterns of decline. When the signal is real, it gently notifies the person you chose to support you.

  • Personal baseline learning over time
  • Mood, sleep, social, and energy signal tracking
  • Sustained-pattern detection (not single bad days)
  • Warm, non-clinical alerts to a chosen support person
  • Full user control over what is shared and with whom
Crisis support

AI Crisis Chatbot

For the 2am moments. A 24/7 conversation that does not judge, does not script, and does not waste your time. Trained on evidence-based crisis-response patterns, it listens first, then connects you to the 988 Lifeline or a trained peer supporter if you want one.

  • Available 24 hours a day, anywhere
  • Direct handoff to 988 Lifeline when needed
  • Connects to a trained peer supporter on request
  • Follows evidence-based safe-messaging guidelines
For friends and family

Letters of Hope

Worried about someone but freezing on the words? Tell us a few things you have noticed. Our AI helps you write a short, warm, honest message that simply says: I see you. I am here. The hardest part of reaching out is starting. We help you start.

  • AI-assisted drafting from a few honest details
  • Trained on warm, non-clinical messaging
  • Always pairs with crisis resources
  • Free to use, no account required
Human connection

Peer Support Network

Trained community members, online and local, ready to show up. Every peer supporter is trained in QPR (Question, Persuade, Refer), the gold-standard short-form gatekeeper training in suicide prevention. Real humans, real conversations, no scripts.

  • QPR-certified peer supporters
  • Available online and in local chapters
  • Optional, never required
  • Ongoing training and supervision
